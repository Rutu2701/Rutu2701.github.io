3-Axis Agricultural Gantry

Three-axis gantry prototype developed as a team platform for targeted irrigation and mechanical weed removal. Work covered motion architecture, mechanical structure, wiring and system integration.

Exploring my capabilities.

ROBOTICSMECHANICSPROTOTYPE

Motion system

Three controlled axes provide positioning over the working area for agricultural operations.

Applications

The prototype was configured for targeted irrigation and mechanical weed removal.

Integration

Mechanical structure, drive components and electrical wiring were assembled as a single working system.

Prototype

The objective was a functional demonstration platform for evaluating motion and agricultural operation concepts.

Engineering focus

The project centred on turning an agricultural task into a controllable spatial-motion problem: define the working envelope, build a three-axis mechanism and integrate the drive and wiring into a testable prototype.
