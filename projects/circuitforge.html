CircuitForge

AI-powered text-to-schematic and PCB compiler for KiCad 7 — an active, vibe-coded experiment in automating parts of electronic design.

Current system

The repository currently describes a pipeline from natural-language circuit description through an AI compiler and netlist representation into KiCad outputs, including schematic, PCB, BOM, Gerbers, STEP and PDF export.
