RESEARCH R02 · AEROSPACE · GNC

Parafoil Target Landing

Design study carried out for the college Rocketry & CanSat Club to assess a steerable ram-air parafoil as the main recovery system for CAN-7USAT. The study was prepared for CDR consideration and later submitted to IN-SPACe.

Scope

Parafoil sizing, glide performance, target-range analysis, guidance law, brake-line actuation, deployment architecture, mass budget, risk and verification.

Selected configuration

0.40 m² planform, 5 cells, AR 2.7, CL 0.75, CD 0.25, 3:1 glide ratio and 2.47 m/s calculated sink rate at the 600 m deployment point.

Guidance

GPS position and course with IMU support; local-frame target bearing and proportional differential-brake control at 5 Hz, followed by symmetric flare near the target or minimum altitude.

Verification

Static inflation, brake-actuation bench testing, low-altitude guided drops, full-altitude testing, GPS-loss testing and flare-timing verification were defined before flight adoption.
